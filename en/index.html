01 / 07Security chip

Layer 01 Foundation

Security chip

Everything starts in the hardware. The keys to your data depend on the Titan M2 security chip and on your PIN. The chip slows down wrong PIN entries with growing wait times. A second PIN, the emergency PIN, wipes the device immediately and irreversibly if you wish, including the eSIM.

LimitA short PIN is guessed quickly. At handover we show you how long it should be.

Layer 02 Hardware checks software

Boot

At every start the device checks, starting from the hardware, that the system is unchanged (Verified Boot). At handover we check the signature together with you, so you can see that nobody changed anything in between.

LimitThe check says the system is genuine. It says nothing about apps you install later.

Layer 03 GrapheneOS

System

GrapheneOS is open source and hardened against attacks. It runs without advertising and analytics services. Updates arrive signed. If the device stays locked for long, it restarts and seals your data again. Apps that demand Google services run in a sandbox if you want them to.

LimitA device without updates ages. Install them when they arrive.

Layer 04 Separate spaces

Profiles

Everyday life and confidential matters run in separate profiles, each with its own apps, data and key. We set up two. GrapheneOS allows up to 32, for example one for travel or one for an app you do not trust. When you end a profile, its apps stop and its key is wiped from memory.

Everyday active · other profiles ended

LimitA profile is separation, not a hiding place. Anyone holding your unlocked device sees the profiles.

Layer 05 You decide

Permissions and radio

Every app gets only what it needs. Camera, microphone, location, network and sensors are released one by one, and one tap switches camera and microphone off completely. NFC and Bluetooth are off from the start, and Wi-Fi and Bluetooth switch themselves off after a while. In every Wi-Fi network the device shows a new address. On a locked device the USB-C port passes power only, no data.

LimitAn app you grant a permission can use it. And your mobile provider still sees where your device is registered.

Layer 06 With Gold

Protected connection

With Gold the confidential profile only goes online through an encrypted connection (VPN, 12 months included) and is also routed through several stations. If the protected connection drops, the profile has no network instead of sending unprotected. With Base you decide per app whether it may go online at all.

LimitThe provider of the encrypted connection sees where you connect to. Anyone who signs in somewhere with their name is still recognisable there.

Layer 07 At the top

Communication

A messenger that needs no phone number, no email address and no user ID. Your contact keys are created on the device and stay there. Messages are end-to-end encrypted and travel through servers that are independent of each other. There is no central account and no directory of all users. With Gold it runs in the confidential profile only through the protected connection, and messages disappear after a set time.

LimitWhoever you write with can read your messages. A screenshot on the other side cannot be prevented.

04On the way

Encrypted on the way.

A message is encrypted on your device and only opened again by the recipient. On the way in between, relay points see nothing but unreadable characters.

In the diagram, messages travel through changing relays. On the way the content stays encrypted. It is readable only on the two devices.

LimitAnyone reading at the start or the end of the route, for example on the recipient's device, sees the message in plain text. Encryption protects the route, not the ends.

Can I use my usual apps?

Most of them, yes. Apps that demand Google services run in a sandbox if you want them to, with normal app permissions. It is best to install them in the everyday profile, not the confidential one.

  • Accounts · always twoPair only

    Security keys

    What does that mean?Two small keys for your online accounts, like a house key and a spare. Without the key nobody gets into the account, not even with your password.

    Learn more
    How it works
    You plug the key into the Pixel (USB-C) or hold it against it (NFC) and confirm the login. A fake login page gets nothing it could use to sign in later.
    What it is good for
    For accounts you want to protect especially well: email, cloud, password manager, bank, as far as they support keys.
    Limits
    You register both keys with your accounts yourself, we never see them. Keep the spare key in a different place. The key protects accounts, not the device, and not every account supports it.

  • Radio · pouch

    Shielding pouch

    What does that mean?A pouch that shields radio signals. Like an envelope made of metal: what lies inside does not transmit to the outside.

    Learn more
    How it works
    The fabric guides radio waves around the device. Mobile network, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and positioning do not reach it as long as the pouch is closed properly.
    What it is good for
    When the device should briefly have no contact: in meetings, on trips, in transit.
    Limits
    The effect depends on workmanship. If the device is not fully inside or the pouch is open, signals get through. We test every pouch before it is passed on.

  • Screen · film

    Privacy screen

    What does that mean?A film for the screen. From the front you see everything, from the side it goes dark. Whoever sits next to you cannot read along.

    Learn more
    How it works
    Fine slats in the film let light through only towards the front. Look at it at an angle and the screen goes dark.
    What it is good for
    For trains, planes, waiting rooms and anywhere others might read along.
    Limits
    Someone standing directly behind you still sees. A film can affect brightness and the in-display fingerprint sensor. We clarify that before it is passed on.

Step 1 of 4Ready

Write safely

Four short steps before you write to us. None of them is mandatory. They help you reveal no more than necessary.

Your own sender address

Set up a free email address that reveals nothing about you: no real name, no phone number, no link to your other accounts. Providers like that still exist.

We deliberately recommend none and link to nothing. Search yourself, for example with DuckDuckGo, and read what details a provider asks for.

With an iPhone«Hide My Email» also works. It is better than your main address but stays tied to your Apple account.

Separate from your data

Do not write to us from your everyday profile, with your cloud account, or with your name in the mail program. The less gets mixed, the less can be linked.

A VPN is an encrypted tunnel. Your Wi-Fi operator or mobile provider then no longer sees where you connect to. The VPN provider sees it instead. So it only shifts whom you trust. It does not make you anonymous.
