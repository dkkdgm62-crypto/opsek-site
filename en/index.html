01 / 07Apps and permissions

Layer 01

Apps and permissions

Every app gets only what it needs. Camera, microphone, location, network and sensors are released one by one. With one tap you switch off camera and microphone for all apps.

LimitAn app you grant a permission can use it. What you install remains your decision.

Layer 02

Communication

A messenger without a phone number and without an account, end-to-end encrypted and ready to use. With Gold, messages are set to disappear after a set time.

LimitWhoever you write with can read your messages. A screenshot on the other side cannot be prevented.

Layer 03

Profiles

Everyday life and confidential matters run in separate profiles, each with its own apps, data and key. We set up two. GrapheneOS allows up to 32, for example one for travel or one for an app you do not trust. When you end a profile, its apps stop and its key is wiped from memory.

Everyday active · other profiles ended

LimitA profile is separation, not a hiding place. Anyone holding your unlocked device sees the profiles.

Layer 04

Radio and cable

NFC and Bluetooth are off from the start, and Wi-Fi and Bluetooth switch themselves off after a while. In every Wi-Fi network the device shows a new address. On a locked device the USB-C port passes power only, no data.

LimitYour mobile provider still sees where your device is registered.

Layer 05

System

GrapheneOS is open source and hardened against attacks. It runs without advertising and analytics services. Updates arrive signed. If the device stays locked for long, it restarts and seals your data again. Apps that demand Google services run in a sandbox if you want them to.

LimitA device without updates ages. Install them when they arrive.

Layer 06

Boot

At every start the device checks that the system is unchanged (Verified Boot). At handover we check the signature together with you, so you can see that nobody changed anything in between.

LimitThe check says the system is genuine. It says nothing about apps you install later.

Layer 07

Security chip

The keys to your data depend on the Titan M2 security chip and on your PIN. The chip slows down wrong PIN entries with growing wait times. A second PIN, the emergency PIN, wipes the device immediately and irreversibly if you wish, including the eSIM.

LimitA short PIN is guessed quickly. At handover we show you how long it should be.

09FAQ

What people often ask.

Your question missing? Write to us, we reply personally.

Ask a question
Can I use my usual apps?

Most of them, yes. Apps that demand Google services run in a sandbox if you want them to, with normal app permissions. It is best to install them in the everyday profile, not the confidential one.

Do you know my PINs?

No. You set all PINs yourself at handover. We do not see them and we store nothing of your content.

What does a device cost?

Tell us briefly what you need it for. You will get a reply with an offer for Base and Gold.

Is there support?

OpSek is a one-time sale. In return you get the manual to keep and access to the community, where questions are welcome.

Do I need technical skills?

No. The device arrives fully set up. At handover we go through the most important steps together.
