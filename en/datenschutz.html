Summary

This website sets no cookies, no trackers and uses no analytics or advertising services. No third-party fonts or scripts are loaded; fonts are served from our own server.

The site is served through GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). According to GitHub's documentation, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. We have no access to this data. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure operation. Data may be transferred to the USA; GitHub's privacy terms apply.

Local storage

The only thing stored in your browser is a note that you have read this notice (localStorage, key opsek-hinweis). It never leaves your device.

Contact
